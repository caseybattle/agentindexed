What NanoParse is for

NanoParse extracts web-page content as Markdown for AI agents. Its Litmus output adds structured signals about the source, freshness, and type of page to help agents assess retrieved material.

Typical use cases

  • Preparing web-page text for AI-agent context
  • Extracting Markdown for research workflows
  • Inspecting structured source-quality signals

How to evaluate NanoParse

  1. Prioritize sandboxing and isolation for code-executing agents
  2. Check cold-start times and scaling behavior for bursty loads
  3. Verify observability hooks integrate with your existing stack
