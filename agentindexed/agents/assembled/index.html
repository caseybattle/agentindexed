What Assembled is for

Assembled sits in the customer support & crm category of the agent stack. Support and CRM agents resolve tickets, draft replies, and keep customer data in sync — deflecting repetitive volume so human teams handle the judgment calls. Leading deployments resolve a large share of inbound conversations autonomously.

Typical use cases

  • Autonomous first-line ticket resolution
  • Drafting replies for human review
  • Keeping CRM records updated from conversations automatically

How to evaluate Assembled

  1. Measure resolution rate on your own tickets, not vendor demos
  2. Check integrations with the helpdesk and CRM you already use
  3. Look for smooth human handoff with full conversation context
