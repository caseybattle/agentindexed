What DeerFlow is for

DeerFlow sits in the research agents category of the agent stack. Deep research agents plan multi-step investigations, browse dozens of sources, and return cited reports — compressing hours of analyst work into minutes. They are becoming standard kit for analysts, marketers, and founders.

Typical use cases

  • Market and competitor research with cited sources
  • Literature reviews and technical deep-dives
  • Due-diligence briefs on companies and markets

How to evaluate DeerFlow

  1. Demand inline citations — uncited research is unusable
  2. Check source freshness and how paywalled content is handled
  3. Look for structured outputs (tables, briefs), not just prose
