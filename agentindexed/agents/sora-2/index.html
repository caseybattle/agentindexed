What Sora 2 is for

Sora 2 sits in the creative agents category of the agent stack. Creative agents generate and edit images, video, music, and design assets — increasingly as autonomous pipelines rather than single prompts. This category covers the tools turning briefs into finished creative work.

Typical use cases

  • Producing marketing visuals and video at scale
  • Automated content pipelines from brief to asset
  • Design iteration and creative exploration

How to evaluate Sora 2

  1. Check commercial licensing on generated output
  2. Evaluate consistency features (characters, brand styles) for series work
  3. Look at editing depth, not just first-generation quality
