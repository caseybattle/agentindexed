Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What we collect

This is a static website. We do not set tracking cookies or run third-party ad trackers. If you submit a listing or start a paid checkout, we store the details you enter in our form (agent name, URL, category, description, chosen plan, your email) in private storage and use them solely to publish and manage the listing and to follow up about your submission or an unfinished checkout.

Payments

Payments are processed entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Stripe's privacy policy governs payment data.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, cookie-free page analytics (Vercel Web Analytics) to count visits. No personal profiles are built.

Your rights

Email casbattle19@gmail.com to request removal of your listing or any personal data we hold about you. We respond within 7 days.
