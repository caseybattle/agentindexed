How we review

Reviewed by the AgentIndexed editorial team · Updated November 17, 2026 · How we review

AgentIndexed is a curated index, not a scraped one. This page explains exactly what happens between a submission and a published listing, and what money can and cannot buy.

1. Submission

Anyone can submit a tool through the submit form. Free and paid submissions go through the same checks.

2. Human review

  1. The product works and is publicly available or has a real waitlist.
  2. The website is the vendor's own, not an affiliate or reseller page.
  3. The description is accurate. We rewrite marketing claims we cannot verify.
  4. The tool fits a category by what it does.

3. Categorization and tags

Each tool gets one category and a small set of tags (language, protocol, deployment). Tags drive the best-of lists, alternatives and comparisons, so they are kept factual.

4. Ordering

Category pages and lists show Featured placements first, clearly badged, then tools with full editorial overviews, then alphabetical order. Search never takes payment into account.

5. Re-checks and removals

Listings are re-checked periodically. Tools that shut down, change category or stop matching their description are updated or removed. Spot something out of date? Email casbattle19@gmail.com.

What paid plans buy

Featured, Pro and Sponsor plans buy position and visibility. They never buy a listing that would not be accepted for free, and they never change search results. See pricing.
